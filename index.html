How it works

From Report to Resolution

Four connected steps keep every complaint visible from the first photo to the final sign-off.

Step 01

Snap & Submit

Take a clear photo and describe what happened. Your report is recorded with photo proof.

Step 02

Ward Review

The issue is assigned to the ward junior engineer and added to the inspection roster.

Step 03

Action in Progress

Corrective work orders are dispatched to site crews and progress is shown in real time.

Step 04

Verified Resolution

Resolved issues are subject to photo verification and citizen sign-off before closing.
